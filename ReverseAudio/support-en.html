How to play

  1. Record a phrase or a bit of a song, up to 15 seconds.
  2. Listen to it backwards, slowed down to 0.5× or 0.75× if you like.
  3. Copy what you hear.
  4. Your copy is flipped back and scored from 0 to 100.

Frequently asked questions

The app can't hear my voice

Open iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone, and turn on Reverse Audio.

My score is always low

Record somewhere quiet with the phone close to your mouth, and listen to the reversed clip at 0.5× before copying it.

Where are my challenges?

In “My challenges”, on your phone. To find them on a new iPhone, turn on “Include in iCloud Backup” in the app's Settings.

Do I need a connection?

No: the app always works offline.

How do I delete my recordings?

Delete your challenges in “My challenges” or uninstall the app: there are no copies anywhere else.

I can't save the video to Photos

Open iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos, Reverse Audio, and allow adding photos.

How many people can play?

Party is for 2–6 players and Chain for 3–6, always on one phone.
