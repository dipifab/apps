In short

Sing Backwards: Reverse Audio does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, servers, ads, tracking or usage statistics: your recordings stay on your iPhone.

Data controller

Fabrizio Di Pietro

Email: support@pediary.net

Microphone and recordings

The app uses the microphone only when you tap the record button. Your voice is reversed and compared with your attempt on the phone itself: the score is worked out on your iPhone and no audio is ever sent to us or anyone else.

The files of a challenge in progress are temporary and are deleted when you close it. Challenges you save in “My challenges” stay in the app's storage until you delete them.

iCloud Backup

Saved challenges are left out of iCloud Backup. If you turn on “Include in iCloud Backup” in the app's Settings, they become part of your iPhone backup, which Apple manages in your iCloud account: we cannot see them.

Videos and Photos

“Make a video” creates the video of your challenge on the phone. You decide whether to share it or save it to Photos, using the iOS share menu. To save it, the app only asks for permission to add items to Photos: it cannot see your photos.

Third-party services

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash reporting libraries and works offline. Apple only shows us aggregated App Store download figures and, if you chose to share them with app developers in your iOS settings, anonymous usage and crash data; Apple's privacy policy applies to that data.

Children

The app is also meant for young people. As it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either, and the videos never show text written by users.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or transfer. To delete everything, delete your challenges in “My challenges” or uninstall the app. You can write to us with any question, and you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the updated date.
